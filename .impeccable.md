# Design context

Audience: a nontechnical founder building a professional cannabis creative agency.
Use cases: manage clients, product-line brand guides and SKU variants; compile production briefs; review research requirements; organize visual assets and revisions.
Tone: precise, contemporary, confident, practical. Neutral cool surfaces, restrained blue accent, sans typography. No cannabis imagery or consumer packaging styling in the studio chrome.
Quality bar: usable first release with truthful integration status and local persistence, backup export, clear errors and responsive editing.
